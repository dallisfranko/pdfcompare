# How to get PdfOverlay on your Windows computer
# (No coding required)

## Step 1 — Download the app

1. Open this link on your personal computer:
   https://github.com/dallisfranko/pdfcompare/raw/cursor/pdf-overlay-poc-6751/releases/PdfOverlay-portable-win-x64.zip

2. Save the file when your browser asks.
   The file name is: PdfOverlay-portable-win-x64.zip

## Step 2 — Unzip it

1. Find the downloaded zip (usually in your Downloads folder).
2. Right-click it → Extract All… → Extract.
3. You should get a folder named PdfOverlay (or similar) with PdfOverlay.exe inside.

## Step 3 — Run it

1. Open that folder.
2. Double-click PdfOverlay.exe
3. If Windows SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”:
   - Click More info
   - Click Run anyway
   (This is common for new apps that are not signed yet.)

## Step 4 — Use it

1. Click Open PDF A… and choose a PDF.
2. Click Open PDF B… and choose another PDF.
3. Move the opacity slider and use the arrow buttons to line the pages up.

## USB for a coworker (like Adam)

1. Copy the whole unzipped folder onto a USB drive.
2. Give them the USB.
3. They still need company IT approval before using it on a work computer.
4. After approval, they double-click PdfOverlay.exe on the work PC / remote desktop.

## Important

- Use your personal computer first.
- Do not put this on a company computer until IT approves it.
- Read IT-REVIEW.md and PRIVACY.md if you need to show IT what it does.
