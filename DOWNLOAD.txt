HOW TO GET PDFOVERLAY ON YOUR COMPUTER
======================================

No coding needed.

1) On your personal computer, open this page (stay logged into GitHub):

   https://github.com/dallisfranko/pdfcompare/blob/cursor/pdf-overlay-poc-6751/releases/PdfOverlay-portable-win-x64.zip

2) Click "Download raw file" and save the zip.

3) In Downloads, right-click the zip → Extract All → Extract.

4) Open the folder and double-click PdfOverlay.exe

If Windows blocks it: More info → Run anyway.

USB for Adam: copy the whole folder to a USB stick.
He still needs IT approval before using it on a work computer.
