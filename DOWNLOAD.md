# How to use PdfOverlay

## Permanent website (GitHub)

After you finish the one-time setup in [WEBSITE.md](WEBSITE.md), open:

**https://dallisfranko.github.io/pdfcompare/**

Then:

1. Choose PDF A  
2. Choose PDF B  
3. Adjust opacity / alignment  
4. Click **Clear everything** when done  

No download. PDFs stay in your browser. They are not uploaded or overwritten.
