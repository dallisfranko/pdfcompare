Web version privacy

This browser tool is designed to keep PDF work temporary and local.

  • PDFs are selected from your computer and processed in this tab only.
  • The page does not upload PDFs to any server.
  • Original PDF files are never overwritten, replaced, or saved back by this tool.
  • No recent-files list is written by the app.
  • Use Clear everything, or close the tab, to discard the session.

Your browser or company security software may still record that a page was opened. This tool itself does not keep your PDF contents after the tab is closed.
